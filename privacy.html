Privacy policy

Liber Copia LLC publishes public-domain history films, videos, podcasts and books. This page covers this website and the software we use to post our own content to social platforms (including the app registered with the Pinterest API).

What we collect

Nothing from you. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics scripts and has no forms or accounts. Podcast feeds and pages are static files.

Our social-media apps

Our apps on Pinterest, YouTube and similar platforms are used only by us, to post our own videos, images and links to our own accounts and to read the statistics of our own posts. They do not access, store or process any other user's data. The access tokens the platforms issue to us are stored on our own computer and are never shared or sold.

Third parties

If you follow a link from this site to YouTube, Pinterest, Spotify or another platform, that platform's privacy policy applies there. We do not receive personal data from them about you.

Children

Our content is general-audience history. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
